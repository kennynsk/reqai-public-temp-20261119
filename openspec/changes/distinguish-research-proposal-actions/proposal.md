## Why

Research can improve existing requirements, but every card looks like an addition and defaults to adding a record. Users cannot tell the intended change and risk duplicates or lost obligations.

## What Changes

- Explicit add/replace intent and a single replacement target in new research proposals.
- New requirement / Change ID labels and before/after wording on cards.
- Matching default action in review, with deliberate user override and reversible application.
- Older proposals remain unclassified and require review; related IDs never imply replacement.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `research-proposal-actions`: Typed research proposals, review and compatible application.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Research prompt and response normalization, proposal cards, review dialog and tests. No migration of historical results or automatic application.
