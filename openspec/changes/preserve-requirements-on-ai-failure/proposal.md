## Why

Model output and audit failures can stop a valid task and leave users without a way to finish reviewing requirements.

## What Changes

- Provide an editable manual registry and an actionable queue of available AI formulations after a decomposition failure.
- Let users edit or finalize the saved input version after a linked research failure.
- Retry malformed research JSON and malformed coverage audit responses within their existing correction budgets.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `requirements-workflow-recovery`: Continue the requirements workflow after a recoverable AI failure.

### Modified Capabilities

## Impact

Temporal workflows, activity handlers, result artifacts and web workspace. Existing result URLs and version creation routes serve the recovery flow.
